DAR FASHION STORE - ANALYTICS INSIGHTS

Key metrics for the assignment demo:
- Total revenue is calculated from the orders table.
- Product performance is calculated from order_items.
- Payment status is tracked in payments.
- Customer growth is tracked from users.
- Newsletter growth is tracked from newsletter_subscribers.

Recommended future improvements:
- Add date filters for daily, weekly, and monthly sales.
- Export reports to PDF or Excel.
- Add charts for revenue, best-selling products, and customer growth.
