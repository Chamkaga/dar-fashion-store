# Marketing Strategy

## Overview
Marketing materials and strategies for Dar Fashion Store e-commerce platform.

## Digital Marketing Channels

### Social Media Marketing
- **Instagram**: Product showcases, fashion trends, customer testimonials
- **Facebook**: Business page with product catalogs, ads targeting local customers
- **TikTok**: Short videos showing product features and styling tips
- **WhatsApp Business**: Customer support, order updates, promotions

### Email Marketing
- **Welcome Series**: New customer onboarding emails
- **Product Launch**: New collection announcements
- **Seasonal Promotions**: Holiday sales, special offers
- **Abandoned Cart**: Recovery emails for incomplete purchases

### Content Marketing
- **Blog Posts**: Fashion tips, style guides, product care instructions
- **Video Content**: Product demonstrations, styling tutorials
- **User-Generated Content**: Customer photos, reviews, testimonials

### Paid Advertising
- **Google Ads**: Search campaigns for fashion-related keywords
- **Facebook/Instagram Ads**: Targeted ads for local audience
- **Influencer Marketing**: Collaborations with local fashion influencers

## Marketing Campaigns

### Campaign 1: Grand Opening Launch
- **Objective**: Generate initial traffic and brand awareness
- **Channels**: Social media, email, local advertising
- **Duration**: 2 weeks
- **Budget**: TZS 500,000

### Campaign 2: Seasonal Sale
- **Objective**: Increase sales during peak shopping seasons
- **Channels**: Email marketing, social media ads
- **Duration**: 1 week
- **Discount**: 20% off selected items

### Campaign 3: Customer Referral Program
- **Objective**: Leverage existing customers to acquire new ones
- **Incentive**: TZS 5,000 credit for each successful referral
- **Mechanism**: Unique referral codes for each customer

## Marketing Metrics

### Key Performance Indicators
- **Website Traffic**: Unique visitors, page views, session duration
- **Conversion Rate**: Percentage of visitors who make a purchase
- **Customer Acquisition Cost**: Cost to acquire a new customer
- **Customer Lifetime Value**: Total revenue from a customer over time
- **Social Media Engagement**: Likes, shares, comments, followers
- **Email Open Rate**: Percentage of emails opened by recipients
- **Return on Ad Spend**: Revenue generated per advertising dollar spent
